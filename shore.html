What's real here, and what isn't yet

  • RealThe shoreline and the islands. Traced from the Lake Ontario outline in OpenStreetMap, , at true scale: .
  • RealThe CN Tower's position and its 553.33 m height. Its shape is a simple marker, not a model of the tower.
  • RealThe footprints of downtown buildings and building parts, from OpenStreetMap, between roughly Bathurst Street and the Don River and north to about College Street.
  • MixedBuilding heights. use a height recorded in OpenStreetMap (white). are estimated from the storey count at 3.2 m per storey (tan). have no height data and are drawn as 6 m blocks (grey). Every block rises straight from the ground, so setbacks, podium gaps and roof shapes are not shown.
  • Not yetGround height. The land is a flat slab a few metres above the water; no terrain has been surveyed in.
  • Not yetBuildings outside the downtown box, streets, and any earlier shoreline. Those are the next layers.
