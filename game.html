    An independent project about Toronto's history. Not affiliated with the City of Toronto or the TTC. Tkaronto is the Kanien’kéha (Mohawk) word the city’s name comes from. It means “where there are trees standing in the water” (The Canadian Encyclopedia). Some facts on this site come from Wikipedia, and each one links to the article it was taken from. Wikipedia can be edited by anyone, and those facts have not been independently fact-checked for this site. Please do not take them as verified.
