            An independent project about Toronto's history. Not affiliated with the City of Toronto or the TTC. Tkaronto is the Kanien’kéha (Mohawk) word the city’s name comes from. It means “where there are trees standing in the water” (The Canadian Encyclopedia). Behind the project: the world so far, the record behind the game, what it was drawn from, and the log.
